# PRODUCT

InsightED PM is an internal project-management tool for about 7 people working across about 15 subprojects under one umbrella project.

- **Users:** one or more Project Managers (plan, govern, see everything) and Team Members (see everything, update only their own work, comment on anything).
- **Jobs:** know what is due this week, next week and this month; keep sprints honest (goal, capacity, one-in-one-out); finish work against a Definition of Done; see portfolio health and who is over their limits.
- **Tone:** plain and direct. Say what happened and what to do next. Errors explain the rule and the way forward (for example which checklist items are missing).
- **Must never feel like:** a surveillance or ranking tool. Workload shows load against limits, never output scores or rankings of people.
- **Not in scope:** multi-tenancy, billing, SSO, email or notifications, real-time sync, file attachments.
